# Privacy Policy for Dripify AI

**Last Updated:** July 15, 2024

Welcome to Dripify AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

We encourage you to read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Email Address**: Collected when you sign in with Apple
- **User ID**: Generated by Apple Sign-In for account identification
- **Profile Information**: Age range and style goals you provide during onboarding

### Content You Provide
- **Photos**: Outfit photos you upload for style analysis
- **Style Preferences**: Your fashion goals and preferences
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the app, including style ratings and feedback

### Technical Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version
- **App Usage**: Features used, time spent in app, crash reports
- **Analytics**: Performance data to improve app functionality

## How We Use Your Information

### Core App Functionality
- **Style Analysis**: Process your photos using AI to provide fashion feedback
- **Personalization**: Tailor recommendations based on your style preferences
- **Progress Tracking**: Save your style ratings and improvement over time
- **Account Management**: Maintain your user profile and preferences

### App Improvement
- **Performance Optimization**: Analyze usage patterns to improve app speed
- **Feature Development**: Understand which features are most valuable
- **Bug Fixes**: Identify and resolve technical issues

### Communication
- **Service Updates**: Important app updates and maintenance notifications
- **Support**: Respond to your questions and provide customer service

## Data Storage and Security

### Supabase Platform
We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our secure backend service provider. Supabase is a trusted platform that:
- Stores all your data in secure, encrypted databases
- Implements industry-standard security measures
- Complies with GDPR and other privacy regulations
- Provides automatic backups and data protection

### Data Protection Measures
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls**: Strict access controls limit who can view your data
- **Regular Security Audits**: We regularly review and update security measures
- **Secure Authentication**: Apple Sign-In provides secure, verified authentication

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

### We Do Not Sell Your Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

### Limited Sharing
We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:
- **Service Providers**: Supabase (our backend provider) and RevenueCat (subscription management)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In the event of a merger or acquisition (with notice)

### Third-Party Services
- **Apple**: For authentication and app distribution
- **Supabase**: For data storage and backend services
- **RevenueCat**: For subscription management (if you purchase premium features)

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- **View Your Data**: You can see your profile information in the app
- **Update Information**: Modify your profile and preferences anytime
- **Delete Account**: Contact us to permanently delete your account and data

### Photo Management
- **Upload Control**: You choose which photos to upload for analysis
- **Storage**: Photos are stored securely and can be deleted upon request
- **Analysis**: Photos are processed for style analysis and then stored for your reference

### Communication Preferences
- **Notifications**: Control which notifications you receive
- **Marketing**: We do not send marketing emails without your consent

## Data Retention

### Active Accounts
We retain your data as long as your account is active and you continue using the app.

### Account Deletion
When you delete your account:
- All personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
- Photos and analysis results are deleted
- Account information is anonymized or deleted
